fix(compiler+csharp-writer): v3 standalone-codegen fixes — substitution chain, paired operators, interface conversions

Three emitter/front-end fixes surfaced by compiling plato-src-v3 generated C# standalone:

- PlatoCompiler/Analysis/TypeSubstitutions.cs: Replace() now chains through concept
  TypeParameter replacements, not just $-type-variables. A concept passing its own
  type parameter to an inherited concept (Field<TDomain,TValue> inherits
  Procedural<TDomain,TValue>) previously leaked raw TDomain/TValue into emitted
  obligations (CS0246 x70, CS0535 x35).

- Plato.CSharpWriter (CSharpFunctionInfo/CSharpTypeWriter/CSharpConcreteTypeWriter):
  when a type declares only one side of a C# comparison-operator pair (v3 Orderable
  has only LessThanOrEquals), synthesize the partner operator by swapping operands
  (CS0216 x86). No-op when both sides are declared (plato-src).

- Plato.CSharpWriter/CSharpConcreteTypeWriter.cs: skip single-field implicit
  converters when the field renders as IReadOnlyList* (conversions to/from an
  interface are illegal C#, CS0552 x86).

Goldens byte-identical (regen-generated 184/184 both variants), frozen V1 clean,
PlatoTests 142/142, conformance 205/205.

v3 standalone error count: 570 -> 293. Remainder is the missing-V3-runtime gap
(Number/Integer/Boolean/String wrapper bridges, primitive-struct obligations for
Vector*/Angle/Quaternion, Curve/Surface Self-constraint cascade), not emitter bugs.
