Plato stdlib, running in the browser

Four demos over the same generated TypeScript. Every polyhedron, ring, boolean and warp below is computed by members that Plato.TypeScriptWriter emitted from the stdlib/ Plato sources — nothing here re-derives the maths.

  • Polyhedra The five Platonic seeds and the Conway operators — ambo, truncate, dual, expand, snub — that name the Archimedean and Catalan solids.
  • Polygons Rings in the plane: area, perimeter, centroid, containment, winding, offsetting and triangulation.
  • CSG Union, intersection and difference of closed solids, by plane-clipping every face fragment against the other solid.
  • Deformers Twist, bend, taper and the rest of the deformation catalog, applied vertex-wise to a dense mesh.
